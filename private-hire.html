The venue

A quiet corner
by the water.

On the ground floor of Ivory House at St Katharine Docks, The Melusine is a light-filled, intimate restaurant with boats outside and the Tower of London nearby.

Chef Theodore Kyriakou’s open kitchen brings guests closer to the cooking. The atmosphere is relaxed and personal, with arrangements shaped around your occasion.
